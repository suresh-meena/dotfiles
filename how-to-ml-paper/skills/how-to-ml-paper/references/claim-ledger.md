Claim types: `Empirical`, `Theoretical`, `Methodological`, `Dataset/benchmark`, and `Analysis/interpretation`.

Statuses: `Supported`, `Partially supported`, `Missing evidence`, `Overstated`, `Citation needed`, and `Outside scope`.

Every substantive abstract and introduction claim must map to evidence, proof, analysis, or an explicit scope statement in the body. Treat a missing verification location as a scientific finding.

For empirical verbs, inspect the strength of the wording. `Observes`, `suggests`, `supports`, and `establishes` imply different evidentiary commitments. Choose the verb from the design and evidence. Treat the sequence as a review prompt rather than a formal scale.
