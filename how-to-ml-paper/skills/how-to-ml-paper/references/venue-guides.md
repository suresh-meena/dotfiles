# Venue routing

Venue rules change. Retrieve current official author instructions, reviewer instructions, template, and checklist for the resolved venue, track, and year. Record the source URL and access date. Treat official requirements as provenance level A.

Check anonymity and self-citation rules, page and file-size limits, template version, appendix policy, supplementary-material policy, required statements, checklists, ethics disclosures, accessibility guidance, artifact rules, and camera-ready validation.

Use official venue sources for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL or EMNLP, CVPR or ICCV or ECCV, and journals. Avoid frozen numerical limits in this skill.
