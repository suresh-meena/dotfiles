# Sources and evidence levels

Access dates below are 2026-09-04 unless noted. Use canonical links and retrieve current venue material when rules may have changed.

## B: peer-reviewed empirical or methodological evidence

- Reimers and Gurevych, [Reporting Score Distributions Makes a Difference](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1035/), EMNLP 2017. Supports repeated-run and score-distribution review for nondeterministic systems.
- Dodge et al., [Show Your Work](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1224/), EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019. Supports compute and hyperparameter-budget reporting.
- Lučić et al., [Are GANs Created Equal?](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/e46de7e1bcaaced9a54f1e9d0d2f800d-Abstract.html), NeurIPS 2018. Supports systematic tuning and restart checks.
- Henderson et al., [Deep Reinforcement Learning That Matters](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/11694), AAAI 2018. Supports variance and significance review in deep RL.
- Musgrave et al., [A Metric Learning Reality Check](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.08505), ECCV 2020. Supports controlled baseline methodology.
- Mosbach et al., [On the Stability of Fine-Tuning BERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04884), ICLR 2021. Supports diagnosis and strong simple baselines.
- Pineau et al., [Improving Reproducibility in Machine Learning Research](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v22/20-303.html), JMLR 2021. Supports reporting checks for runs, hyperparameters, splits, uncertainty, compute, assumptions, and proofs.
- Kim et al., [Threads of Subtlety](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.298/), ACL 2024. Supports discourse-structure variability as a corpus-level signal.
- Russell et al., [People who frequently use ChatGPT for writing tasks are accurate and robust detectors of AI-generated text](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.267/), ACL 2025. Supports lexical and broader stylistic cues in human detection for the tested models and corpus.
- Saha and Feizi, [Almost AI, Almost Human](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1303/), ACL Findings 2025. Supports caution around AI-polished human text.

## C: researcher-authored practice

- Foerster, [How to ML Paper](https://www.jakobfoerster.com/how-to-ml-paper). Core X/Y/Z/verification structure and reader-oriented workflow.
- Langley, [Crafting Papers on Machine Learning](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2002/craft.html), ICML 2002 essay. ML evaluation, communication, and noun-phrase guidance.
- Steinhardt, [Advice for Authors](https://jsteinhardt.stat.berkeley.edu/blog/advice-for-authors). Precision, consistent terminology, context, and early payoff.
- Eisner, [Write the Paper First](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/write-the-paper-first.html). Early experimental design, empty tables, and empty plots.
- Parikh, [Planning Paper Writing](https://deviparikh.substack.com/p/planning-paper-writing-553f497e8839). Claim-centered Results skeleton.
- Forbes, [Figure Creation Tutorial](https://maxwellforbes.com/posts/figure-creation-tutorial-making-a-figure-1/). Iterative Figure 1 design and local interpretability.
- Freeman, [How to Write a Good Research Paper](https://deviparikh.com/citizenofcvpr/static/slides/freeman_how_to_write_papers.pdf). Direct technical openings, baselines, ablations, fairness, and figures.
- Durand, [Notes on Writing](https://people.csail.mit.edu/fredo/PUBLI/writing.pdf). Reader load, sentence structure, and layered explanation.
- Li, [Writing Tips](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~tzli/writing_tips.html). X/Y/Z argument framing.

## D: provisional and local heuristics

- Bitton et al., [Detecting Stylistic Fingerprints of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01659), arXiv 2025. Model-family stylometry; peer-review status unverified here.
- Repeated paragraph geometry, redundant closure, negative anaphora density, triad density, and uniform rhetorical polish remain revision heuristics.
- Lipton and Steinhardt, [Troubling Trends in Machine Learning Scholarship](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03341), 2018. Methodological criticism supporting separation of observation and speculation; it is not controlled empirical evidence for a detector.

The cited detection studies predate rigorous comparison of Claude Opus 5, Claude Fable 5, and GPT-5.6. Product availability does not establish a prose fingerprint. Never attribute authorship or model identity from these rules.
